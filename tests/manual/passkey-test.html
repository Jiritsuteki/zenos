ZENOS passkey test

A test of signing ZENOS approvals with a passkey kept in the phone's secure chip (iPhone: Secure Enclave; Android: the phone's hardware key store). Nothing is sent anywhere: the keys stay on the phone, and the results are shown on this page only.

電話の安全なチップに入るパスキーで ZENOS の承認に署名できるかの試験です。どこにも送信しません。結果はこの画面にだけ出ます。

1. Make a test passkey / 試験用のパスキーを作る

On the phone: Face ID or fingerprint. On a PC: the browser offers "use a phone"; scan the QR code with the phone.

2. Approve a sample request / 見本の承認に署名する

The signature is bound to the request: which PC, which file, who, when. It is checked on this page.

3. From a PC, with the phone nearby / PC から、近くの電話で

Open this page on a PC. The PC shows a QR code; the phone reads it and the two check over Bluetooth that they are near each other. From far away it does not go through.
